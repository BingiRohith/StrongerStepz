Introducing

Welcome to Stronger Steps.

We are Dr. Nikhil Mehra and Dr. Akhila, two doctors passionate about helping people age with strength, confidence, and independence.

At Stronger Steps, we believe that growing older should not mean giving up the activities you love. Through evidence-based exercise, nutrition guidance, and healthy aging education, we help adults stay active, improve mobility, build strength, and maintain their independence.

Our mission is simple: to help you take stronger steps toward a healthier, more active life—at every age.

Because healthy aging isn’t about adding years to life; it’s about adding life to years.
